**Drivers, in the model's order of influence:**
1. Spending Momentum Index: pushes the forecast up
2. Transaction-data signal (licensed third-party card panel): pushes it up
3. Fuel prices: pull it down
4. Consumer confidence: pushes it up
5. Holiday calendar (Thanksgiving timing): pushes it up
6. Baseline trend and other factors: push it up

These are model attributions. They show how each input moved the forecast inside the model, not proven causes of sales growth. Your Standard tier includes the direction and rank of each driver but not their size in percentage points.
