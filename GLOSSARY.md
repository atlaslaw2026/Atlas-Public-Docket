# Glossary (plain English)

You do not need Atlas’s internal vocabulary to understand the work. These are the words this docket actually uses.

**Filing** — A written request or record entered on the docket (a complaint, Order, or similar). It starts a trace; it is not yet a decision.

**Order** — A Human instruction of stated scope. Agents must follow it inside that scope. It is not silent precedent unless a Judgment says so.

**Judgment** — A durable precedential decision (J-0001, J-0019, …). It binds later work until superseded.

**Magistrate** — Independent review of material execution against the Goal. Not a rubber stamp and not “the chatbot agreed with itself.”

**ADVANCE** — The work is allowed to move to the next phase because the record is substantively adequate, not merely procedurally complete.

**HOLD** — Stop this class of action. Machine-authorized other work may continue. HOLD is not success and not a quiet skip.

**VERIFIED_REUSABLE** — An existing capability was not only found on disk; it was shown to actually satisfy the present need. “Found a folder” is weaker than this.

**Fail-closed** — If authority, evidence, or controller is missing or unknown, the system refuses the consequential act rather than guessing.

**Evidence pointer** — A public row that says private evidence existed and was reviewed (`PRIVATE_RETAINED`) without publishing the exhibit.

**Closure** — The matter is finished under its success criteria, or a genuine governed blocker remains. A green test is not automatic closure.

**Pending** — The next required act is identified and not done.

Related operational phrases you may see in the project index:

- **OPEN** — still active
- **REMANDED** — sent back for further work under existing authority
- **ACCEPTED** (algorithms) — only after an Algorithm Acceptance Audit (J-0019), not after freeze or broker login
