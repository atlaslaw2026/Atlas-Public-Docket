# Architecture (public)

Atlas is a governed AI operating loop, not a single chatbot.

1. **Goal** — what the Human is trying to accomplish (Dkt. 1 / operative Goal pointer).
2. **CLINTON_SDK** — Human-role surface: directives vs working context. Not an Atlas Room.
3. **Librarian** — what Atlas *has* (catalog of records). Catalog is not law.
4. **Atlas Room** — what Atlas *can do* (capabilities). Catalog presence is not availability. Discovery does not grant execution.
5. **O-GC-002** — before building a new capability, discover and reuse; replacement needs a recorded mismatch or Human override.
6. **Magistrate** — independent review of material execution against the Goal (J-0020, J-0021).
7. **Jobs / docket** — durable work records. Derived CURRENT_STATE is operational memory, not law.
8. **Evidence** — sourced claims, hashes, pointers. Private exhibits stay in the private companion.

Rooms, SDKs, Watchers, Hunters, and calculators are capabilities available to the intelligence. They are not a substitute for thinking, and required first hops are not optional ceremony.

The Entra-protected Human docket viewer is a **lens** over the private record (J-0011). This public GitHub docket is a **sanitized portfolio/procedural surface**, not that lens and not a second live host.
