## AD-3. EFFECT OF MAGISTRATE DISPOSITIONS

AD-3.1 **Effective when filed.** A Magistrate disposition takes effect when it is filed, within its lawful scope. Neither an appeal nor a pending petition suspends it. [Art. VI §7; VESTED L16]

AD-3.2 **Self-stay or self-condition only by express order.** A Magistrate may stay her own disposition, or condition reliance on it, only by an express order that:
(a) is labeled STAY or CONDITION;
(b) states the reasons;
(c) states what ends it; and
(d) is filed on the Docket.
A condition stated in any other way has no suspending effect. [Art. VI §7]

AD-3.3 **Immediately appealable.** Any party, or the Human, may appeal a self-stay or self-condition at once, without waiting for finality. [this Order]

AD-3.4 **Non-Magistrate findings.** A finding by a certifier, auditor, or other actor has no power of its own to void, stay, or delay a disposition. Such a finding may support an appeal or a petition. [V1 Supplement 1 L39; Art. VI §7]

AD-3.5 **Validity questions.** Whether a purported disposition is valid (properly constituted, within jurisdiction, and authentic) remains a question of law. It may be raised by appeal. [VESTED L16; D2 H6–H7]

## AD-4. JURISDICTION OF THE APPELLATE DIVISION

AD-4.1 **Final dispositions.** Appeals as of right from any final Magistrate disposition. [Art. VI §6]

AD-4.2 **Interlocutory rulings.** Appeals from interlocutory rulings by leave of the Appellate Division, except as AD-3.3 provides. Leave is granted where an immediate appeal may materially advance the matter or prevent material harm. [Art. VI §6]

AD-4.3 **Petition to compel a decision.** See AD-10. [Art. VI §6]

AD-4.4 **Excluded matters.** The Appellate Division has no jurisdiction over:
(a) the Human's own acts;
(b) questions reserved to the Human;
(c) the content of law; or
(d) matters no Magistrate has decided, except under AD-10.
[Art. I §1–§2; Art. XVIII §1; Art. VI §3]

## AD-5. COMPOSITION, INDEPENDENCE, AND INSTANCE IDENTIFICATION

AD-5.1 **Panel.** Each appeal or petition is heard by a panel of three Magistrate instances, each freshly and independently instantiated for that appeal only. [Art. VI §6; VESTED §I L10]

AD-5.2 **Exclusions.** The following may not sit on a panel:
(a) the instance whose disposition is under review;
(b) any instance previously seated in the same matter;
(c) any instance spawned with a party's or spawner's conversation as context; and
(d) any agent that is a party or advocate in the matter.
[Art. XIV §1; VESTED L12, L32]

AD-5.3 **Seating record.** Before a panelist acts, Atlas preserves:
(a) the panelist's charter, read-only;
(b) the spawn prompt, with its sha256;
(c) the panelist's own conflict disclosure, stating exactly what context and instructions it received; and
(d) a setup certification by an actor that is not a party and not the spawner.
Every later input to a panelist is saved with its sha256 before delivery. [Art. XIV §1; VESTED §VII–§VIII]

AD-5.4 **Instance identification.** Each panelist identifies itself and its platform task id in every filing. An appellate disposition names each panelist and how it voted. [VESTED §VII L64]

AD-5.5 **Recusal and replacement.** A panelist with a disclosed or discovered conflict recuses. Another fresh instance replaces it under AD-5.1 to AD-5.3. [Art. XIV §1]

AD-5.6 **Decision.** Two concurring panelists decide. Each panelist may file a separate opinion or dissent. [this Order]

AD-5.7 **No instruction.** No party, spawner, or the Human may instruct a panelist what to hold. That rule does not limit Art. I §2. [OHSM L201–205]

## AD-10. PETITION TO COMPEL A DECISION (duty to decide)

AD-10.1 **Duty.** A Magistrate who has jurisdiction over a question properly before her has a duty to decide it. She may decline only where Atlas law withholds jurisdiction or reserves the question to the Human. Where she declines, she must say so expressly. [Art. VI §2; O-MAGISTRATE-ACTIVATION L66; VESTED L28]

AD-10.2 **Grounds.** A petition lies where a Magistrate with jurisdiction:
(a) fails to decide a question properly before her within a reasonable time;
(b) declines to decide without stating a lawful ground; or
(c) conditions or suspends her own decision other than by an order under AD-3.2.
[Art. VI §6–§7]

AD-10.3 **Relief.** The panel may:
(a) order the Magistrate to decide;
(b) vacate a non-conforming condition or suspension; or
(c) where the Magistrate can[status mark removed for public reading copy]fully act, refer the question to a fresh instance.
The panel does not decide the underlying merits on a petition unless the parties consent. [Art. VI §6]

## AD-11. STAYS AND FINALITY

AD-11.1 **No automatic stay.** Filing an appeal or petition does not stay anything. [Art. VI §7]

AD-11.2 **Stay on motion.** The panel may stay a disposition pending appeal only by express order with reasons. It weighs four factors:
(a) a strong likelihood of success;
(b) irreparable harm without a stay;
(c) harm to others; and
(d) the interest of Atlas.
[Art. VI §7; this Order]

AD-11.3 **Finality.** A disposition is final when either:
(a) the time to appeal runs out without an appeal; or
(b) the appellate disposition is filed, subject to AD-11.4.
[OHSM L244; this Order]

AD-11.4 **En banc.** **[HUMAN CHOICE — included by default]** Within 72 hours of an appellate disposition, a party may request rehearing by a new panel of three fresh instances. AD-11.5 **Human sovereignty untouched.** Finality under this Order does not limit the Human's power to amend, supersede, strike down, or reserve by a later Human act. [Art. I §2]
