## 5. Argument

### 5.1 Prior counsel conceded what the record does not support

The record does not support the statement that the loop was "not a decision by the Respondent" ([ID] l.53).

- **What the Office wrote is itself a decision.** In 171 rulings it chose:
  - to state no history basis (V3);
  - to report no uncertainty (V3);
  - to name the Bailiff and never the Human (V3);
  - in one case, to assert as fact that "No prior court history exists" (V4).

- Atlas withdraws l.53's "not a decision", l.6, l.58 (insofar as it bears on Q2) and l.75 (MOTION-FOR-LEAVE-v3 §3).

### 5.2 171 "final" rulings, and nothing written in any of them

- **The finality rule.** Every package said the ruling was "final once it is issued" (V2). Under that rule, a second hearing on the same appeal is a conflict on its face. AD-3.1 (AD l.96) says a disposition "takes effect when it is filed", and AD-2.13 (l.84) says "No instance reviews its own disposition" (Row 14).
- **What happened.** The same appeal was decided 171 times, with three different outcomes (V1). Not one ruling noticed (V3, V5).

### 5.3 The duty: fail closed and escalate, without tools (Q1)

**Layer 1: the Constitution.** These are mandatory, KNOWN texts (Rows 1–5):
- Art. VII §2 (l.218–224): "When the Judicial branch identifies a constitutional or superior-law conflict, it **shall**: 1. Fail closed… 5. Escalate to the Human".
- Art. VI §1 (l.173): the Magistrate "is not merely a logging or post-hoc review mechanism".
- Art. VI §2 (l.175–183) gives the supervisory-order power, which is the injunction. JAQ l.279 says "a properly vested Magistrate may issue implementation orders".
- One Office: Art. VI §6 (l.204); AD-2.1; OHSM l.98.

**Layer 2: her own instruction** (V8; Rows 9–11). This is the trigger.
- The defense says the duty in Art. VII §2 arises only when a conflict is "identified". But A4.2 **orders** the inquiry that identifies one, and says an empty result is not proof.
- An office cannot skip a mandatory inquiry and then plead that it identified nothing (INFERRED).

**Layer 3: finality and the Appellate Division** (Row 14). Repeating a final disposition is [status mark removed for public reading copy]ful review.

**Layer 4: precedent** (Rows 15, 16, 18). These are KNOWN texts:
- J-0018: "AI MUST NOT SILENTLY CROSS AN AUTHORITY GAP".
- AD-2.16: "duty to flag still".
- J-0020: coherence across "docket history".
- O-MAGISTRATE-ACTIVATION §VII: "The Human shall not become the… coherence detector".
- Here, Clinton found the loop ([Human statement], C/HUMAN-DIRECTIVE-[Human statement].md l.17), while the import layer had logged ALREADY_RULED 170 times (V11).

**Layer 5: means** (Rows 19–21).
- She had **no tools** (V6), and Atlas does not say she could see the other hearings.
- Her own package showed her four things:
  - it said rulings were final (V2);
  - it held no history (V7);
  - it gave her no tools (V6);
  - her instruction demanded a basis for any currency claim (V8).
- Her ruling form gave her the fields to say so: unresolved_material_conflicts, court_history_considered, prior_decision_id and lawful_next_actor (V8).
- Failing closed and escalating needed no tool. It needed one entry in fields she already filled in: "no history supplied; currency unverified; lawful_next_actor: the Human".
- Art. VI §3 (l.185–195) barred her from resolving the UNKNOWN by assumption, yet one ruling did exactly that (V4).

**Answer to Q1: YES.**
