 PROJECT 1-1 — FINAL HUMAN SUMMARY
 Project 1-1 asked whether a named ten-stock Space universe could be researched honestly, priced without corporate-action
 fiction, and compared under frozen rules—then watched forward on paper instead of being endlessly redesigned. The
 names are RKLB, ASTS, IRDM, PL, SPIR, VSAT, GSAT, LMT, LHX, and LUNR.

 Atlas split the work into rooms that must not collapse into one another: Hunter gathered filings and company evidence;
 Atlas-Math calculated; strategy judgment froze rules; execution placed only paper or shadow marks; verification replayed,
 audited, and docketed. Mixing those rooms is how a reverse split becomes a fake fortune.

 What was built, without overclaiming: a Hunter corpus with remaining IR gaps; Atlas-Math with a verified current pass
 and a later corporate-action repair; a corrected Passive baseline; frozen ALG1-V1 (monthly equal-weight of the same ten
 names); historical AI-ALG2-V1 and AI-ALG2-V2.1 tests (not the live forward AI book); qualitative company research; a
 forward trio (Passive shadow, ALG1 Alpaca paper, AI-CHALLENGER-V1 shadow); GitHub unattended paper cycles;
 and a docket that later had to confess it had looked in the wrong GitHub repository for the scheduler.
