## 3. The claim, in plain words

The box has a hard file limit (inodes). Atlas law says that at 80% full, big test runs stop. Once the box crosses that line, work does not slow down. It stops, one workflow after another, like dominoes: tests are held, builds are held, the off-box copy is held, and a new project waits. That is a machine failure that anyone watching could see coming, and the record shows Secretary saw it coming.

Secretary was the bot Clinton gave the job of getting the files off the box and onto his D: drive. Secretary measured the box, forecast in writing when it would run out, and ran the copies to D:. Even so, she did not make sure the files actually left the box, did not finish the D: copy, built no software and pushed for none to keep the box under the line, and did not tell Clinton that the line was stopping everyone's work. She admitted that last point herself today (§6, F17).

Clinton asks the Magistrate to find that Secretary breached her duty to foresee and prevent this, to order the software fix (the space wheel) finished by set dates, and to hold that a failure that is foreseeable and repeats needs software, not memory.
