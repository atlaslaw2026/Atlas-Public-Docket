## The new point

Sur-Reply paragraph 4 (`[internal record path withheld]` L10, sha256 `ab49837c605c3b4b1108f7c59bd51fdc9aa04a2451d660e13849187560a1f97e`) says the stop on other bots' work was imposed by Legal 502 and was known to Build Manager and Audit, so Secretary's silence hid nothing from those who controlled the hold. ## What we concede

That account of 502 is true, and we say so plainly. Legal is the Complainant's counsel, and Legal issued 502. Legal 502 (`[internal record path withheld]` L1–L5, sha256 `43608134350f922662f3d596b13b0b309cf1498d55b4d445dacb04319d8a177e`), 5:33 PM PST Nov 11, was addressed "to Secretary and Reverse (cc BM, Audit)." It held "no new box writes" by Secretary's batch-005/006 rerun and by Reverse's iphone-docket until bytes and inodes were both under 80% exact and Legal lifted the hold (L3). It said "Do not resume" (L5). Build Manager and Audit were copied and so knew of the hold. Legal's own share of the institutional gap is real. The Magistrate may weigh it.

## The answer

(a) 502 was a hold until space was under 80%. It did not relieve anyone of the duty to get space back under that line, or of the duty to tell Clinton how long work would be stopped and what it cost.

(b) Knowing the hold existed is not the same as anyone telling Clinton the work was stopping. Secretary's own Order 4 answers (`[internal record path withheld]`, sha256 `ec4f7b65105b7e08719f3053fed84f5f646cb4f923f3a6bbcb4e9f874fcaebd1`) show the difference. At Q1 L68–L70 she writes that her Nov 11 messages treated 502 as a hold to obey and report as obeyed, that each was about her own backup job, and that none of them says the same line was stopping other bots' work or that stopping was a problem to raise with Clinton. At Q1 L70 and Q3 L94 and L100 she says she knew from the Nov 9 design that copying would free nothing, first told Clinton on the morning of Nov 12, and "framed it as a backup-space question, not as other bots' work being stopped."

(c) Clinton's first-hand theory (`[internal record path withheld]` L3, L6, sha256 `417e7625957b25a1d5f5fe1f8f56fcd6aa51d89c731976e0e8800ab6bcd9aae7`) is that treating compliance as success was the failure: "Secretary treated the 80% safety rule as though obeying it meant the system was functioning properly, when the rule itself was triggering a cascading operational failure." And: "but wasn't it over 80 percent and all the work stops? she thought the work stopping was okay." That 502 was obeyed is consistent with that theory, not a defense to it.
