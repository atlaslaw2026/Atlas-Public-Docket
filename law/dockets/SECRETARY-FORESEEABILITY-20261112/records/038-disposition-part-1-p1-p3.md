## 6. Reasoning
**P1(a), from 11:41 AM Nov 9.** P.1 binds "every Atlas AI in its area" (O.1). Secretary's area includes the Nov 9 backup she owned (F-3). It does not include box infrastructure, which M.2 gives to Build Manager, or the 80% warnings, which O-BOX-SPACE-001 point 2 assigns to the watcher. "Via Secretary" makes her the route for reports to Clinton. It does not transfer other actors' duty to foresee onto her.

Inside her own area the duty is plain. From Nov 9 the box was already over the line (F-2). She was reminded on Nov 10 (F-4) and held on Nov 11 (F-5). She had already forecast exactly this failure (F-1). A copy-only design could not bring the box back under the line (F-3), and she concedes she should have raised that (concession iii). P.1 required her to anticipate that her own backup would leave the box stuck, and either act or report it to Clinton. She did act inside the no-delete limits (F-4, F-5), and she did not hide anything. But she did not report the foreseeable result, that her backup could not free space, until Nov 11 or 12 (F-7). That is a lapse of P.1 in her own area.

Her silence about other bots' stalls (F-6, concession ii) is a real gap, but in Atlas law it belongs first to the watcher and to Build Manager. Legal imposed the 502 hold on Reverse, and Build Manager and Audit knew of it. Clinton's own diagnosis ([scrubbed: turn id]) describes a habit across the institution of treating obedience to the 80% rule as if the system were fine. On this record that is shared, not hers alone.

**P1(b), before 11:41 AM Nov 9.** Rules P and O are prospective (L18, P.2). Before then only the general duty in J-0002 applies. Under it she forecast the failure, warned Clinton, and raised the keeper gap openly for his decision (F-1). D8's "move" instruction conflicts with D9 and with the Oct 26 stop (F-9). A breach cannot rest on a CONFLICTED instruction.

**P2.** Point 3 binds "the bot doing it" when work Clinton is waiting on is blocked. Her own blocked work was reported: the hold was obeyed and reported, and the batch-005 failure was reported to Clinton at 7:45 PM PT Nov 11 (STAGING_RECORD L40). The stalls of other bots' work were not hers to report under point 3. Her statement at F17 is a candid account given after the fact, and the Notice (para 5) said it would not be counted against her. It is not an admission that she breached point 3. The record shows no stall of her own work that stayed silent past 15 minutes. The time of her first message to Clinton after 502 (transcript position 1102) is INFERRED as Nov 11, so the 15-minute window is UNKNOWN to the minute. The burden is on the complaint, and it is not carried.

**P3.** The last specific instruction (D9) asked for a backup. The no-delete laws barred removing originals without Clinton's specific yes. The copy-then-delete order came only on Nov 12, and it went to Ship ([scrubbed: turn id]). Clinton's [scrubbed: turn id] shows what he wanted, but it does not prove an earlier instruction, which is CONFLICTED (F-9). So no duty to move (that is, to remove originals) is proved. The duty that is proved is the copy backup she owned, and it is unfinished: batches 001, 002 and 004 OK, batch-003 fixed, batch-005 failed, batch-006 held under 502. That goes to relief in Part 2.

## 7. Disposition
- **P1(a): SUSTAINED IN PART, narrowly.** From 11:41 AM PST Nov 9 to Nov 11/12 2026, within her own area (the Nov 9 D: backup), Secretary did not anticipate and report to Clinton that her copy-only design could not bring the box under the 80% line while that line was stopping work. That was a lapse of Rule P.1. Otherwise P1(a) is NOT SUSTAINED: she had no personal duty under P or O to keep box space for the whole box, or to page for other bots' stalls. Those duties sit with the watcher, Build Manager and each blocked bot. The failure is found to be shared across the institution.
- **P1(b): NOT SUSTAINED.**
- **P2: NOT SUSTAINED.**
- **P3: NOT SUSTAINED as pleaded.** No duty to move originals off the box is proved. The unfinished copy backup she owned is carried to Part 2 (relief).
- **No sanction.** None was asked and none is imposed. Her candor and concessions are noted in her favour.
