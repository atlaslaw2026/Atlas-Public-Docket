## Rule

`atlas_gov.consult` SHALL include the operative Constitution pointer among canonical sources and SHALL NOT treat the Goal as a source of missing procedure.

Consult precedence after ratification (lookup only; not a second NEXT_ACT chooser):

1. Atlas Constitution (`law/constitution/CURRENT_OPERATIVE_CONSTITUTION.json` → `ATLAS_CONSTITUTION.md`)
2. Explicit Human authority of applicable scope
3. Operative Goal (purpose and objective selection only)
4. Atlas Code if any operative provision exists; otherwise incorporated standing gates
5. Canonical docket filings of the correct series (`docket/ATLAS_DOCKET.json`, `docket/ATLAS_GOVERNANCE_FILINGS.json`)
6. Uncodified ACTIVE Judgments, implementing Orders, latest Magistrate review
7. Derived CURRENT_STATE — operational memory, not law

O-MATERIAL-UNCERTAINTY-CANONICAL-CONSULT remains historical adopting text for the consult *function*. This Order updates precedence. If the two Orders are read to conflict, this later Order controls precedence; the earlier Order still controls forbidden sources, ceilings, and “consult is not a second Magistrate.” That tension is recorded in the conformity conflict finding, not silently erased from the earlier file.
