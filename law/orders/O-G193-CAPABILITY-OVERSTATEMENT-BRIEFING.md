## I. PURPOSE

G-193 recorded the existence and capability state of the Atlas V2 shared SDK layer.

Subsequent direct source-code inspection established that G-193 characterized those capabilities as more complete and general than the implementation actually supported. G-194 corrected the present capability characterization while preserving G-193 as the historical filing.

The Court requires briefing on: what happened; and how Atlas should prevent a cloud agent from making the same kind of unsupported or overstated capability claim again.
