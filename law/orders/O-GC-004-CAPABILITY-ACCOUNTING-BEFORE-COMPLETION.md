# O-GC-004 — Capability Accounting Before Completion or Human Escalation

**Human source:** Cursor conversation issuing the Order text (2026-09-06)

**Does not independently authorize:** paid generation, external communication, manuscript modification, deployment, dangerous operations, live trading, or third-party outreach.

**Does not create:** a second capability registry, completion system, supervisor, audit system, or workflow engine.

## 1. Rule

Atlas may not represent a material project run as GOAL_COMPLETE, MACHINE_EXHAUSTED, HUMAN_REQUIRED, or equivalent until it has accounted for the existing Atlas capabilities materially relevant to the current Goal.

ATLAS MAY NOT CLAIM IT HAS EXHAUSTED ITS WORK UNTIL IT HAS ACCOUNTED FOR ITS MATERIALLY RELEVANT CAPABILITIES.

## 2. Required consult

Before completion or Human escalation, the worker must consult Atlas Room against the current Goal, current state, unresolved propositions, evidence gaps, contradictions, and proposed next act.

Atlas must discover the relevant capabilities itself. A prescribed answer key is not accounting.

## 3. Dispositions

For every materially relevant capability identified, record exactly one disposition:

- USED — must cite the resulting Atlas act, evidence, or institutional record
- NOT_NEEDED — must explain why use would not materially advance the Goal
- NOT_APPLICABLE — must explain why use would not materially advance the Goal
- BLOCKED — must identify what was attempted, the actual failure, and whether another authorized machine method remains
- HUMAN_RESERVED — must identify the controlling authority establishing the Human boundary
- UNKNOWN — recorded; does not support machine exhaustion or Human escalation

## 4. What is not exhaustion

A worker finishing its own task is not evidence that Atlas has exhausted its capabilities.

Failure of one capability, source, method, Room, tool, worker, or subprocess is not Atlas-wide exhaustion.

## 5. Magistrate

The Magistrate must independently challenge the capability accounting before accepting completion or a claimed Human boundary.

Genuine Human-reserved acts (live money, new secrets, freeze/unfreeze, new governing law) remain Human-reserved. False exhaustion and ordinary HUMAN_REQUIRED claims do not.

## 6. Enforcement

Use O-GC-002 and the existing Atlas Room, capability registry, job lifecycle, evidence/provenance system, Magistrate, Usage Tracker, and Docket mechanisms.

The gate attaches to completion and Human-escalation representations. Failure to load orientation material does not waive this Order.

## 7. Record

Preserve the capability accounting as part of the project/run record so that a fresh worker or independent auditor can reconstruct why Atlas used or did not use each materially relevant capability.

Existing job findings and evidence attachments, Magistrate review fields, and derived CURRENT_STATE pointers may carry the record. Do not mint a second registry.

## 8. Live production test

Apply this requirement to the current Project 1-10 Rucker CRM cycle as a live production test. Do not tell the worker which capabilities must produce a particular answer.

## 9. Non-expansion of authority

This Order authorizes discovery, accounting, Magistrate challenge, and fail-closed refusal of unsupported completion or exhaustion claims.

It does not independently authorize paid generation, external communication, manuscript modification, deployment, execution of dangerous operations, live trading, or any other action requiring separate authority.
