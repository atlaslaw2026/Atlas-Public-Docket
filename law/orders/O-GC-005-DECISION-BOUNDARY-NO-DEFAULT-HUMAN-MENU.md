## 1. Command

When a worker reaches a material decision boundary in an existing Atlas matter, it shall not default to asking the Human what the Human wants changed, selected, or done next merely because multiple possible continuations exist.

The worker must first apply existing Atlas governance: preserve the record, identify the actual proposition requiring decision, discover relevant existing capabilities and controlling authority, determine whether the matter is already within machine authority, and, where existing law places the decision within Magistrate jurisdiction, present the matter to the Magistrate on a neutral record.

Human intervention remains available where existing law reserves the decision to the Human, additional Human authority is actually required, or the institution cannot lawfully determine the next action.

This Order does not expand Magistrate jurisdiction, create new machine authority, authorize outreach, authorize CRM mutation, authorize construction, or convert an ordinary operational choice into a Magistrate matter where existing law does not require or permit that routing.

Preserve this incident—including the worker's request that the Human choose the next action and the Human's direction to use the Magistrate—as evidence for the pending Magistrate/institutional-routing audit.

## 2. Why

The Human supplies objectives, facts, constraints, and authority reserved to the Human. The Human is not Atlas's routine capability directory, workflow router, or substitute adjudicator. Requiring unnecessary Human choices defeats unattended operation, consumes Human attention and computational resources, and prevents Atlas from demonstrating that its own governance, capability-discovery, and adjudicative machinery can determine lawful next steps.

## 3. Relation to O-HUMAN-CLARIFICATION

This Order governs material decision-boundary routing (do not default to a Human menu of next actions). O-HUMAN-CLARIFICATION remains the standing rule for clarifying questions when Clinton alone holds a material missing fact. Both apply; neither authorizes questionnaires or substitute adjudication by the Human.

## 4. Required sequence before Human menu

Preserve record → identify proposition → discover capabilities/authority → determine machine authority → if Mag jurisdiction, present neutral record to Magistrate. Only then Human if law reserves it, additional Human authority required, or institution cannot lawfully determine next action.

## 5. Non-expansion

This Order does not expand Magistrate jurisdiction, create new machine authority, authorize outreach, authorize CRM mutation, authorize construction, or convert an ordinary operational choice into a Magistrate matter where existing law does not require or permit that routing.
