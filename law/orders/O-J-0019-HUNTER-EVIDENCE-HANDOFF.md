# O-J-0019-HUNTER-EVIDENCE-HANDOFF

**Filing type:** USER ORDER  
**Date:** 2026-08-26  
**Implements / supplements:** J-0019, O-J-0019-REMEDIABLE-EVIDENCE-REMAND  
**Does not authorize live money.**  
**Does not create execution authority from a remand.**

## Rule

Hunter shall not satisfy a remediable-evidence remand by returning a narrative conclusion to the originating room.

Hunter shall produce and canonically record the evidence generated during the remand. Each material item shall be classified quantitative, qualitative, mixed, or unresolved, and shall record provenance, source, methodology, hashes where applicable, validation disposition, and a canonical filing reference.

Quantitative claims and computed results shall be evaluated through Atlas Math validation. Qualitative factual or methodological claims shall be evaluated through Qualitative validation. Mixed evidence shall be split or validated through both. Strategy Design may rely only on evidence that has completed the required validation and filing path.

Hunter shall continue targeted research on the remanded proposition when additional reasonably obtainable evidence remains available, rather than returning an unchanged conclusion after an identical search.

Stop only when the proposition is resolved; the evidence is non-remediable, unavailable, unauthorized, or outside scope; or the governed record supports that no additional available research under the current scope is likely to resolve it.

Preserve already PROVEN work. Block duplicate identical research loops. Do not route EXECUTE, PAPER_FORWARD, broker, or fill while J-0019 is NOT_ACCEPTED.
