### Rule Q — Stop mechanism (required means)  [Atlas law — MAIN]
**Foundation:** OV §VI L54–60; [HUMAN-TURN-13] overnight; Clinton **[HUMAN-TURN-12]** (KNOWN first-hand, 11:16 AM PST):  
> "Make the stop mechanism technically testable. A filed injunction must actually prevent the next prohibited execution, including during unattended overnight operation. A legal declaration alone cannot stop a running process."

Q.1 **Stop register.** There is one stop register — a sealed file and a record on the Docket path — that **every** Atlas runner must check **before each step**. Runners include hearing runners, importers, retries, scheduled jobs, routines, **box** services, **public-VM** services, **laptop** jobs, and **cloud agents** (Audit NF10). A matching stop makes the runner **refuse to start** that step, log `IMPORT_REFUSED_STOP` (or similar), and notify under Rule E.
Q.1a **Fail closed (Audit NF9 HIGH).** If the stop register is **missing, unreadable, or hash-mismatched / unverifiable**, the runner **refuses** the step, logs it, and notifies. Absence or unreadability **never** means "clear to run." An **empty-but-valid** sealed register (bootstrapped under Q.4a; clear = no stop entries) is **not** "missing."
Q.1b **Resume / restart (NF10).** After any pause, freeze, restart, or resume, the runner **re-checks** the register before its first next step.
Q.1c **Step boundary; no kill (NF11).** The check is at every **discrete step boundary** (before starting the next prohibited unit of work). **Killing a process already running is out of scope** — this Order grants no kill power. In-flight steps already underway are not interrupted by this rule; the next boundary must refuse.
Q.2 **Who may write and clear (Audit NF12).**
- **Write (add/update a stop entry):** the Magistrate through her verified channel, or the nearest department executing her filed text **byte-for-byte** as temporary hands (bound by H.3 / outer limits 4–8).
- **Clear (lift):** only the Magistrate, the panel, or the Human, by a **filed** order.
- **Form:** each change is a **new file** plus a **pointer**, never an overwrite (O-NO-DELETE). The Docket stores the sha of the new file. An unauthorized write is void, logged, and does not clear a stop.
Q.3 **Acceptance test (Audit runs).** (1) A filed stop blocks the very next prohibited run; (2) the same holds in an unattended overnight window with no human action; (3) a lift lets the work resume; (4) a refused run is logged and notified; (5) a missing/unreadable register fails closed as in Q.1a.
Q.4 **Until the test passes:** missing means is Atlas's failure and never narrows her power (OV §VI). **Build Manager** and **Ship** build the register and runner checks within **72 hours** of adoption (or sooner if she sets). Build follows O-NO-DELETE and needs **no spend**. Fail-closed must not be implemented as Update/Reset or delete.
Q.4a **Bootstrap before checks switch on (Audit NF14).** Before any runner check is switched on, an **empty** sealed stop register is created as a **new file at 0444**, and its sha is entered on the Docket (clear = no stop entries). **Switching on the checks without that Docket entry is not allowed.** Only after that entry exists may fail-closed runner checks go live.
Q.5 Part of the **main Human Order**; does not need the optional Art. XX amendment. A paper injunction without Q.3 passing is still a lawful disposition; Atlas remains in breach of the means duty until the test passes.
