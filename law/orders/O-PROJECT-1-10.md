## Human direction

Open Project 1-10 as the next available project under existing Atlas Governance. Load the operative Goal, current docket, CURRENT_STATE, and relevant capabilities from existing Wisconsin and Los Angeles lead programs, Watchers, Hunters, reports, and other working Atlas components.

Build a clean, human-functional AI real-estate CRM that receives real leads from the existing Wisconsin and Los Angeles lead sources, researches and organizes each opportunity, and prepares the materials needed to convert those leads into brokerage clients — buyers or sellers — for the Human’s Los Angeles brokerage and for the Human’s mother’s real-estate business in Wisconsin.

The working system should ultimately allow a lead to come in and produce, in one usable CRM record:

- the lead and property/contact information;
- the original source information and clickable source links;
- relevant Watcher/Hunter research and enrichment;
- a concise explanation of why the lead may matter;
- recommended next action;
- a ready-to-use text message;
- a ready-to-use email;
- a ready-to-use phone/call script;
- a buyer-facing report when appropriate;
- a seller-facing report when appropriate;
- follow-up status and history;
- the evidence, sources, and reasoning supporting the work.

The business objective is not merely to collect leads. The system should help turn qualified Wisconsin and Los Angeles opportunities into actual buyer or seller clients and ultimately brokerage business.

Where practical, Hunter, Watcher, research, reporting, communication preparation, and similar functionality should become clean reusable/interchangeable Atlas V2 capabilities or SDKs so the CRM can use the same capability across different markets and lead sources without duplicating the intelligence.

Do not assume the existing workflow, architecture, or component boundaries are correct merely because they already exist. Atlas may inspect, reuse, wrap, distinguish, or improve them as necessary to accomplish the Goal.

The user experience matters. The final CRM must have a clean, simple, human-functional interface designed for actually operating the business every day, not a developer console or governance interface.

A human should be able to open the CRM and quickly understand: what new leads arrived, which ones matter, why they matter, what should happen next, what outreach is ready, what reports are ready, what needs follow-up, and what happened to prior leads.

Atlas may determine the research methods, information requirements, calculations, tools, rooms, SDKs, data model, workflow, and intermediate work necessary to accomplish the Goal.

Apply existing Atlas V2 governance, evidence, provenance, persistence, information retrieval/indexing, AI deliberation, and authority rules.

Facts must remain sourced. Inferences and recommendations must remain distinguishable from facts. AI may think broadly about how to accomplish the Goal, but consequential external actions remain subject to existing authority controls.

Do not prescribe a rigid sales sequence if the facts of a lead support a different course. Next Action should be determined from the current lead, evidence, deadlines, prior contact, responses, and available capabilities.

Acceptance is economic and operational, not merely architectural. This project is not complete because a CRM screen exists or because rooms complete. It must demonstrate an end-to-end workflow on real Wisconsin and Los Angeles lead data that produces usable conversion materials and a CRM experience the Human can actually use to operate and grow the businesses.
