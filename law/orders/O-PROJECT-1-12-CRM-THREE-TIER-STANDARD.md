## Ruling

Human directive, given in session, verbatim in substance: *"Every mature CRM deal should be capable of producing Level 1 Intelligence, Level 2 Internal Brokerage Operations, and Level 3 External Party Collateral, with all three derived from the same controlled record and no downstream product permitted to invent new facts."*

This rule applies to Atlas Real Estate CRM Project 1-12 work across lead types, counties, and matter types (judicial foreclosure, non-judicial trustee's sale, probate, and others not yet encountered). It binds any agent operating under Atlas's own institutional process (Hunter, Librarian, ATLAS_MATH, QUALITATIVE, Magistrate). It does not and cannot bind an AI tool used outside that process — this Order has no authority over a different agent's own operator-level rules or an unsupervised session that does not consult Atlas's docket.

## 1. The three tiers, defined

**Level 1 — Intelligence.** The controlled evidentiary record for the deal. Contains, at minimum:

- Hunter-bound evidence: every material proposition traced to a literal, sourced extract with provenance (SHA-256, human-viewable source, hunt ID), not paraphrase.
- Legal authority research: the actual governing statute(s) or process for the matter type, quoted or cited to a primary source, not assumed from a prior lead's pattern.
- ATLAS_MATH calculations: ratios, margins, timelines, and commission scenarios, each showing its derivation and its inputs' evidence class.
- Qualitative analysis: explicit FACT / CALCULATION / SUPPORTED INFERENCE / UNKNOWN / AUTHORITY classification for every material claim, including any known defect in a tool or data source that was found and corrected during the work.
- Strategic synthesis: an honest recommendation that follows from the record, including what is NOT established and what would kill or change the deal.

**Level 2 — Internal Brokerage Operations.** Working documents for the Human principal's own use: an Internal Deal Brief, a Homeowner/Seller Outreach Plan where a party to address actually exists, and a Buyer Opportunity Brief. Every fact in Level 2 must already appear in Level 1. Level 2 may add operational judgment (who to call first, what to ask, what would kill the deal) but may not introduce a new fact absent from Level 1.

**Level 3 — External Party Collateral.** Polished, one-page Seller and/or Buyer Reports intended as genuine brokerage marketing collateral aimed at moving the property — not attorney-referral content, not neutral-informational content, and not legal advice. Level 3 leads with the most evidenced, controlling fact (a deadline, a timeline compression, a margin) and uses inferences drawn from the Level 1 record to make the strongest honest case. Level 3 must not assert a fact, an owner identity, or a relationship between parties that Level 1 flagged as UNKNOWN or NOT ESTABLISHED.

## 2. Single controlled record; no downstream invention

All three tiers derive from one Level 1 record. A fact may not appear in Level 2 or Level 3 unless it traces back to Level 1. Where Level 1 could not establish a fact needed for a normally-expected Level 2 or Level 3 document (for example, no Homeowner Outreach Plan is possible without an identified homeowner), that document is omitted and the omission is stated, not filled with an invented or assumed fact.

## 3. Independent Magistrate review required

No package is complete or eligible for delivery to the Human principal until it has received independent Magistrate review under existing J-0020 authority, using a fresh Argument/Counterargument/Magistrate invocation chain. A package with a Magistrate disposition other than ADVANCE, or one requiring `user_decision_required`, is not complete.

## 4. Visual quality assurance required — AMENDED 2026-09-07

The Human-approved Rucker, Anderson, Trapnell, and Malibu finished outputs (Section 5) are the demonstrated production-quality reference set for every tier they cover. A worker may adapt substance and structure to the particular deal, but the finished artifact must meet or exceed the demonstrated visual quality, clarity, organization, usability, and professional presentation of those Human-approved outputs.

This is not a content requirement alone. It applies to the rendered artifact itself, independent of whether the underlying facts and analysis are correct.

Before any Level 2 or Level 3 file is treated as ready for delivery, the producing AI shall:

1. Render every page of the actual output file (not the source HTML/template) to an image.
2. Visually inspect each rendered page for overlap, orphaned headings, pagination defects, header/footer artifacts, and any defect that would not appear in the reference corpus.
3. Compare the result against the reference corpus in Section 5 for visual quality, clarity, organization, and professional presentation — not merely technical validity.
4. Repair any visible defect and re-render, repeating this cycle until the product is genuinely presentation-ready.

A technically valid PDF or HTML file — one that opens without error and contains the correct facts — is not sufficient on its own. Every AI producing CRM collateral through Atlas Project 1-12 inherits this standard automatically via this Order and the reference corpus it points to (Section 5, `CRM_INTELLIGENCE_REFERENCE_STANDARDS.json`). Clinton should not have to separately instruct a new worker to "make it look like those PDFs" — this Order and the reference corpus are the instruction.

## 4a. Default visual system and page architecture — AMENDMENT 2, 2026-09-07

The Human-approved reference corpus (Section 5) establishes both the substantive quality bar (Section 4) and the **default publication layout**. Future CRM products shall use the approved visual system and page architecture unless the particular evidence or audience materially requires a deviation. Any deviation must preserve or improve readability and professional quality. Workers may not redesign CRM collateral merely for novelty.

The actual, bug-fixed source template for each of the six documents is durably stored at `Atlas-Real-Estate-CRM/evidence/crm/project_1_12/templates/` (see that folder's `README.md`), so a worker copies and adapts rather than re-deriving the visual system from scratch or from screenshots. Reuse this location before designing anything new, per `O-GC-002`.

## 5. Demonstrating examples

This standard is evidenced by four worked Project 1-12 leads, registered in `Atlas-Real-Estate-CRM/evidence/crm/project_1_12/CRM_INTELLIGENCE_REFERENCE_STANDARDS.json`:

- **STD-1-12-RUCKER-LA-PROBATE** — 620 Bay Street, Santa Monica. CA probate / IAEA authority, Prop 19, TitlePro evidence chain. Level 1 only (pre-dates this Order's Level 2/3 requirement).
- **STD-1-12-ANDERSON-WI-FORECLOSURE** — 719 Madison St, Mineral Point, WI. Wis. Stat. ch. 846 judicial foreclosure. First full three-tier package.
- **STD-1-12-TRAPNELL-WI-FORECLOSURE** — S7415 Red Mound Ave, De Soto, WI. Second WI foreclosure; thin-margin, compressed-timeline lead; demonstrates honest calibration when the equity story is weak. Full three-tier package, Reports 7-12 in `D:\CRM_Reports`.
- **STD-1-12-MALIBU-PCH-TRUSTEE-SALE** — 21201 Pacific Coast Hwy, Malibu, CA (T.S.# 88954). CA non-judicial trustee's sale; OCR-only source with an unresolved owner identity; demonstrates the standard adapting Level 2/3 scope (no Seller Report, no Outreach Plan) when a required fact is genuinely UNKNOWN rather than inventing one. Level 1/2/3 package, Reports 13-16 in `D:\CRM_Reports`.

These examples demonstrate required depth, discipline, and honest adaptation — they are not rigid universal templates. A future lead's actual facts govern its own document set, per the existing governing principle already on file in `CRM_INTELLIGENCE_REFERENCE_STANDARDS.json`.
