## Ruling

Nobody gets to say "done" until someone's tried to prove them wrong.

The worker's capability accounting under O-GC-004 is the Argument for completion. Before the magistrate accepts completion, a genuinely independent Counterargument must attempt to defeat it: a separate session, working from the record alone, tasked with proving the worker quit too early. Per G-201 practice, a Counterargument is not genuinely independent if it comes from the same session that produced the accounting.

This Order is the standing Human authorization required by J-0018 for that track. The track opens automatically with every capability accounting. No per-run permission is required.

Where Atlas runs as the Human's personal agent, the Human is the challenger. The agent must present its accounting in plain words — what it used, what it skipped, and why — and "done" stands only if the Human accepts it or declines to challenge. The explanation must be complete enough that a later session can verify the work instead of trusting it.

A challenge that approves every disposition without adversarial testing is void and does not satisfy O-GC-004 §5. A pattern of unchallenged NOT_NEEDED dispositions across runs is flaggable as a governance failure.

IT IS SO ORDERED.
