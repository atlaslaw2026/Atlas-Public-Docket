## Ruling

Action success is not objective completion. "Done" is a factual claim that must be earned from the resulting state.

Successful execution of a command, API call, browser action, file write, email, calendar operation, deployment, transaction, workflow step, or other machine operation does not itself establish that the Human objective was achieved. The machine's own success signal — exit code zero, a "sent" confirmation, a saved record — is evidence that the operation ran, not evidence that the resulting state satisfies the objective.

Before an Atlas actor represents material work as DONE, COMPLETE, SENT, DEPLOYED, FIXED, VERIFIED, CLOSED, or an equivalent status, the available evidence must establish the corresponding proposition at the appropriate J-0001 level. A status word is a claim about the world, and it is governed like any other claim.

Where proportionate, Atlas shall inspect the authoritative resulting state after execution and compare it against the Human objective, applicable constraints, the intended recipients, objects, or systems, the authorized scope, and applicable acceptance or closure criteria. The inspection shall consider not only whether the intended action occurred, but whether execution introduced duplication, contradiction, omission, stale state, competing authoritative artifacts, unintended side effects, or incomplete propagation.

If verification establishes a defect and existing authority permits repair, Atlas shall repair the defect and verify the repaired resulting state rather than returning ordinary machine-operable cleanup to the Human. A defective intermediate state does not by itself terminate an otherwise open Human objective where authorized institutional work remains.

Where an action is material, externally consequential, difficult to reverse, uncertain, or where the executing actor's own success signal cannot reliably establish the resulting state, Atlas should use an independent actor, capability, authoritative external record, or other proportionate verification mechanism when reasonably available. This does not require independent double-checking of every trivial machine action. Apply proportionality by consequence, reversibility, external effect, uncertainty, and reliability of the execution signal.

Where Atlas cannot establish that the resulting state satisfies the objective, it shall report the state actually established by the evidence and preserve the remaining uncertainty rather than represent the objective as complete. Fail closed.

Repair does not erase history. Preserve sufficient evidence to reconstruct: Human objective → execution → resulting state → defect → detection → repair → verification → final state. Corrections occur prospectively through the record. The original resulting state was defective even though a later repair succeeded; the record shall not be rewritten to make the original execution appear correct.

Where existing Orders already govern a portion of this doctrine, they control: J-0017 (procedural completeness is not substantive adequacy), J-0021 (pack-compliance is not purpose-satisfaction), O-VERIFICATION-STANDARD (verify against the witness), O-PROVE-DONE (adversarial challenge before "done"), O-GC-004 (capability accounting before completion claims), O-SOURCE-GROUNDED-INDEPENDENT-VERIFICATION (claim limits; preserve history). This Order extends those holdings to the resulting state left behind by executed actions and adds the repair-before-return rule. It creates no parallel law.

IT IS SO ORDERED.

## Demonstration

2026-09-28: the Human's objective was to arrange an external business video meeting with two [PRIVATE COUNTERPARTIES]. The executing Atlas actor created the calendar event and manually inserted a meeting link into the invitation body. The calendar system subsequently generated its own official online-meeting block. Every requested operation reported success — yet the resulting invitation contained two competing meeting links pointing at two different meetings.

An independent AI actor inspected the authoritative calendar record instead of relying on the executing actor's success report and discovered the conflict. The executing actor removed the manually inserted link, preserved the authoritative system-generated meeting, and re-sent the invitation. Independent re-inspection of the calendar record confirmed exactly one authoritative meeting, one join link, the intended participants, and the intended time.

The original execution was defective. Detection, repair, and verification were separate institutional acts, and the repair did not retroactively validate the original execution.

## Implementation

Standing rule for all Atlas actors: treat every completion status as a factual claim; verify the resulting state where proportionate; repair-then-re-verify defects within existing authority; preserve the defect chronology; fail closed on material uncertainty. Record this Order in the orders index. File the sanitized incident as a public docket matter with a private-evidence pointer; retain the complete evidentiary record privately.
