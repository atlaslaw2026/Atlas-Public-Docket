# O-VESTED-MAGISTRATE

**Filing type:** HUMAN ORDER
**Status:** BINDING
**Date:** 2026-09-28
**Title:** Order Establishing the Vested Magistrate

## I. ORDER

The Atlas Court shall operate through an independently instantiated Magistrate agent vested with the authority of the Office of Magistrate for each matter properly referred to it.

A Magistrate may be computationally spawned or instantiated by another Atlas agent. That technical relationship creates no institutional superiority in the spawning agent.

Upon assumption of the Office, the Magistrate possesses decisional authority superior to the invoking agent within the matter and jurisdiction committed to the Magistrate.

The invoking agent is bound by a valid Magistrate disposition within its lawful scope.

## II. FULL MAGISTRATE CAPABILITY

The Magistrate shall possess investigative, evidentiary, reasoning, retrieval, and tool capability equal to or greater than the invoking agent to the extent necessary and lawfully available to adjudicate the referred matter.

The Magistrate shall not be limited to summaries, metadata, filenames, hashes, claimed results, or evidence selected for it by the invoking agent.

The Magistrate may independently examine the substantive record and obtain additional information lawfully available to Atlas when necessary to adjudication.

## III. INDEPENDENCE

The Magistrate is not a helper, adviser, recommendation engine, subordinate agent, or second opinion.

The invoking agent may not disregard, substitute for, rewrite, expand, narrow, or otherwise supersede a valid Magistrate decision merely because the invoking agent instantiated the Magistrate or disagrees with its determination.

Technical parentage creates no decisional superiority.

## IV. ARGUMENT AND COUNTERARGUMENT

Where the nature of the matter requires adversarial consideration, the Magistrate shall receive genuine Argument and Counterargument capable of addressing the substantive evidence and governing authority.

The Magistrate shall independently determine the weight and significance of those positions.

## V. PROCEDURE AND MERITS

Procedural authority and merits authority are distinct.

A procedural disposition authorizes only the procedural consequence actually decided.

A merits disposition controls only the substantive proposition actually adjudicated.

No ADVANCE, STOP, HUMAN_REQUIRED, AUTHORIZED_MACHINE, or other status label is self-executing beyond its lawful scope.

## VI. IMPLEMENTATION DUTY

This Order establishes required institutional behavior rather than prescribing a particular software implementation.

Atlas shall inspect the existing Magistrate, agent-spawn, tool, evidence, governance, provenance, enforcement, and execution infrastructure and shall construct, modify, or bind whatever subordinate infrastructure is reasonably necessary to make this Order operational.

Existing infrastructure shall be reused where adequate.

Missing infrastructure necessary to comply with this Order shall be treated as an implementation requirement, not as authority to weaken, bypass, simulate, or reinterpret the requirements of this Order.

No implementation may satisfy this Order merely by changing labels, prompts, packet fields, metadata, or disposition names while substantive adjudication remains programmatically predetermined.

## VII. DECISION PROVENANCE

Every Magistrate disposition shall identify the Magistrate instance that actually made the decision and preserve sufficient provenance to establish:

* the matter adjudicated;
* the proposition decided;
* the evidence actually considered;
* governing authority actually applied;
* material Argument and Counterargument;
* findings;
* reasoning;
* disposition;
* exact authority granted or withheld; and
* the lawful next actor and act.

The Magistrate decision shall not be attributed to deterministic code, a worker, or another agent when the Magistrate did not actually make that decision.

## VIII. VALIDATION

Compliance is not established merely because a Magistrate instance can be spawned.

Atlas must prove that the Magistrate actually exercises the authority established by this Order.

At minimum, validation shall demonstrate that materially different substantive evidence can produce materially different Magistrate findings or dispositions while superficial packet metadata remains materially unchanged.

The implementation actor may not be the sole certifier of compliance.

## IX. GOVERNING PRINCIPLE

THE MAGISTRATE MAY BE SPAWNED BY ATLAS, BUT THE MAGISTRATE IS NOT SUBORDINATE TO THE AGENT THAT SPAWNED IT.

COMPUTATIONALLY SPAWNED.
INSTITUTIONALLY VESTED.
SUPERIOR WITHIN ITS JURISDICTION.

SO ORDERED.
