# Consumer Grocery Tracker — methodology change log

**2024-01** — Tracker launched. Monthly national sample of U.S. adults 18+, target ~2,500 completes. Mixed-mode fieldwork: approximately 40% computer-assisted telephone interviews (CATI) and 60% self-administered online. Results weighted to Census benchmarks on age, sex, region, and education. Published figures are weighted; `n` is the unweighted count of completes.

**2024-07** — Added a module on delivery-fee sensitivity (not included in this data room). Core question wording unchanged.

**2025-04** — Weighting targets refreshed to the latest ACS estimates. Internal re-weighting check showed impact on pct_online_primary of less than 0.2 points in any month.

**2025-11** — Minor wording change to an unrelated question on store-brand purchasing. pct_online_primary wording unchanged.

**2026-03** — Fieldwork transitioned to an online-only, probability-recruited panel; the CATI telephone component was retired. The question wording and weighting scheme are unchanged. A parallel bridge wave was fielded in February 2026 in both the legacy mixed-mode design and the new online panel design (see `bridge_study_2026-02.csv`). The published tracker series is **not adjusted** for the transition; the Methods team is evaluating whether an adjustment is required. The February 2026 tracker figure is from the legacy design.

**2026-08** — August wave fielding in progress. Figures are preliminary, based on a partial sample, and will be replaced at final close (expected 2026-10-06).
