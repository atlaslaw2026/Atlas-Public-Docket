# Methodology summary (client-visible)

**Apparel Purchase Intent Survey.** A monthly online survey of about 3,000 U.S. adults, weighted to Census benchmarks. The headline measure, `intent_pct`, is the percent of adults planning to buy apparel in the next 30 days. Margins of error (`moe95_pts`) are 95% intervals that include a design effect for weighting. At the national level they are about ±2 points; for regions they are about ±3–5 points.

**Spending Momentum Index (SMI).** A proprietary Halvorsen composite of survey intent and transaction-data signals, indexed to 100. The construction and weights are proprietary.

**HX-APP forecast.** Halvorsen's proprietary model of U.S. apparel retail sales, year over year. The published outputs are point forecasts with 80% prediction intervals. Driver attributions show how much each input moved the forecast in the model. They are model attributions, not measured causal effects.

**Brand trackers (custom).** Client-commissioned studies of brand consideration. Results belong to the commissioning client.
